# Fourwright — Brand Guide

**Name:** fourwright (always lowercase in the logo; "Fourwright" in sentences)
**Tagline:** Said straight. Built right.
**Descriptor:** Data & AI engineering

## The idea
*Wright* is an old English word for a builder (shipwright, playwright): Fourwright builds things right.
The symbol is a pinwheel made of four bars turning around one shared center: different parts, one direction.
The "four" lives only in the name and the symbol. Don't explain it or build copy around it on the website or in marketing.

### Rules
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the width of one bar.
- Minimum size: symbol 24 px wide on screen, 10 mm in print.
- Don't stretch, rotate, re-color the bars individually, add shadows or put the logo on busy photos.
- The amber bar always stays amber, even in the white version.

## Voice
- Talk like a 30-year-old expert: clear, confident, no jargon for the sake of it.
- Concrete: dollars, hours, risks. Never vague promises.
- Close to younger people without forcing slang.
- **We are:** top quality at a fair price, calm, careful. **We are not:** greedy, cheap, rushed or sloppy.
- English first, Spanish second.
